Mohamed Kande

Kande, who was an electrical engineer, is now a consultant who is set to become the PwC's next global chair, and also the very first black person to lead the Big Four firm. Kande was raised in the Ivory Coast with his mother, who is half-lebanese in a catholic and Muslim family before moving to France at age 16. He struggled with the challenges of France's far right extremist encounters and random ID checks for black and brown people. But he was able to voercome all that through getting a MBA from the University of Chicago and a degree in electrical engineering from the University of Montreal, and the ESIGELEC in France. His story tells about the greatness of black men overcoming adversity.

Safiya U. Noble

Safiya Noble is the acclaimed author of Algorithms of Oppression: How Search Engines Reinforce Racism and a co-editor for both The Intersectional Internet: Race, Sex, Class, and Culture Online and Emotions, Technology and Design. Noble attended California State University and studied Sociology, which undoutebly works to shape her commitment to investigating digital inequity. She received her M.S. and Ph.D. in Library & Information Science from the University of Illinois at Urbana-Champaign. Not only is she an academic hero to many, but her work in dismantling digital harassment and exploring how technological and search algorithms reinforce and maintain racism, also situates her as an important leader in the tech world as well.
