Mohamed Kande

Kande was raised in the Ivory Coast with his mother, who is half-lebanese in a catholic and Muslims family before moving to France at age 16. He struggled with the challenges of France's far right extremist encounters and randome ID checks for black and brown people. But he was able to voercome all that through getting a MBA from the University of Chicago and a degree in electrical engineering from the University of Montreal, and the ESIGELEC in France.
